Basis and assumptions

  • Sec 185 exemption (private companies, Notfn. GSR 464(E) as amended 13-06-2017): no body corporate in share capital; borrowings from banks, FIs or bodies corporate below the lower of 2× paid-up capital or ₹50 crore; no repayment default subsisting.
  • Sec 185(2): where the exemption is not available and the borrower is a private company in which a lender’s director is a director or member, a Special Resolution is needed and the loan must be used for the borrower’s principal business.
  • Sec 186 applies to every loan: unanimous Board resolution, limit of the higher of 60% of (paid-up + free reserves + securities premium) or 100% of (free reserves + securities premium), interest not below the G-sec yield for the closest tenor, register MBP-2.
  • Deemed dividend, Sec 2(40)(e) of the Income-tax Act 2025 (formerly Sec 2(22)(e)): a shareholder holds 10% or more of voting power in the lender and 20% or more in the borrower, or the borrower itself holds 10% or more in the lender. Taxable up to the lender’s accumulated profits.
  • Entities not in the shareholding list, LLPs and firms are excluded from the matrix until their holding or partner details are available.
  • This page supports, and does not replace, a legal and tax review of each loan.

Look-through beneficial ownership by individual

Each individual’s holding is traced through every corporate shareholder: direct % plus (corporate holder’s % in the company × individual’s % in that holder), repeated up the chain. For example, Jaya Dealers: Yashaswi Shroff 14.47% direct + 71.06% (Alcove Construction) × 49.40% = 49.57% effective. Holdings that cannot be traced to a named individual are shown as unidentified. Hover a cell for the breakdown.

Beneficial ownership detail and SBO assessment

One row per company and individual, with the holding path. SBO (significant beneficial owner, Sec 90 and the SBO Rules 2018) applies where an individual holds 10% or more indirectly, or indirectly together with direct holdings. Where the member is a company, the indirect holding counts if the individual holds a majority stake (more than 50%) in that member or its ultimate holding company, or exercises control or significant influence, alone or acting together.
